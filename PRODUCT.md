# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Victoria Mincheva's existing and prospective social-media followers, arriving primarily on mobile from TikTok or Instagram. They are beauty-conscious shoppers looking for an approachable way to recreate Victoria's makeup and quickly find products she genuinely uses and recommends.

## Product Purpose

Create a polished home for Victoria's beauty recommendations that converts social attention into useful action. The primary commercial goal is to sell her $4.99 Full Glam Guide through a clear, trustworthy purchase flow. The secondary goal is to make her affiliate product catalogue easy to browse by product type and easy to shop without overwhelming visitors.

## Brand Personality

Soft, polished, trustworthy. Victoria should feel like a beauty-savvy best friend with excellent taste: aspirational enough to inspire, warm enough to believe, and direct enough to act on.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic link-in-bio button stacks, loud pink beauty templates, hard-sell dropshipping language, fake urgency, fabricated social proof, cluttered marketplaces, and cold technology-brand styling. The experience should never feel anonymous, discount-led, or manipulative.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with Victoria's real routine and point of view, not a generic digital product pitch.
2. Make the guide the unmistakable hero while letting the affiliate catalogue reward further exploration.
3. Earn trust through specificity, transparent expectations, clear pricing, and honest persuasion.
4. Treat mobile attention as precious: keep paths short, controls thumb-friendly, and choices easy to scan.
5. Use restraint as a luxury signal, with purposeful motion and generous space rather than ornamental clutter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain strong contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, useful alternative text, 44px touch targets, and a complete reduced-motion experience. Never rely on color or animation alone to communicate meaning.
